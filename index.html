Community Aid

Find real help in your most difficult moment

This section connects people with concrete help in their community. It doesn't just inspire: it also guides towards food, clothing, nearby support and real resources for difficult times.

  • Find help near you
  • Access real and useful resources
  • Receive support with a clear experience
  • Connect faith with concrete action

Community Aid resources are informational third-party references. In an emergency call your local emergency number (911 in the US) or 988 for emotional crisis support.

Planned

Optional membership for the Spiritual Counselor

We plan a membership option for extra access once the complimentary reflections are used. It is not active yet, and there is no date or price. On iOS it would be handled through Apple (in-app purchase).

Prayer cards that match their prayer

Each daily prayer card will open and play the exact prayer it shows, so title, text, reference and audio always match. It is not available in the app yet.
