Light of Life EN ES PT Privacy Policy Last updated: June 4, 2026 Light of Life ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you use our platform, including our mobile application, website, and related services. 1. Information We Collect We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve the Light of Life experience. This includes: Account Information. When you create an account, we collect basic identifying information such as your name or display name and email address. Additional profile information is always optional. Prayer Submissions and Community Content. Content you submit to the Prayer Wall or other community features — including prayer requests and testimonies — is collected and may be visible to other users as intended by the feature. AI Companion Interactions. When you interact with our AI Spiritual Companion, the content of those interactions may be processed to generate responses. We do not use AI interaction content for advertising purposes, and we do not sell it to third parties. Donation and Payment Information. When you make a donation, payment processing is handled by Stripe, our third-party payment provider. Light of Life does not store your full payment card details. We may receive confirmation of transaction amounts and identifiers for legally required record-keeping. Device and Technical Information. We automatically collect certain technical information when you use the platform, including device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, and general usage patterns. This information helps us maintain platform performance and security. 2. How We Use Information We use collected information only for the purposes below. We do not sell your personal information to third parties. • To operate and maintain the platform and its features • To provide prayer support, community features, and AI companion responses • To process donations and maintain required financial records • To respond to support requests and user inquiries • To enforce community guidelines and ensure platform safety • To improve the platform experience and resolve issues • To comply with applicable legal obligations 3. Third-Party Service Providers To deliver our features, we share limited data with the following trusted third-party providers. Each operates under its own privacy policy and data processing agreements. • OpenAI — The Spiritual Counselor feature sends the text of your prayer queries to OpenAI's API to generate AI-assisted spiritual guidance. OpenAI processes this data on servers located in the United States. We do not transmit your name, email address, or account identifiers to OpenAI. OpenAI Privacy Policy → • ElevenLabs — Our text-to-speech feature converts prayer text and spiritual guidance responses into audio using ElevenLabs' API. The text content of prayers and AI responses is transmitted to ElevenLabs for voice synthesis processing. ElevenLabs Privacy Policy → • Supabase — Your account information, prayer requests, testimonies, and community data are stored and managed using Supabase infrastructure, hosted on AWS servers in the United States. Supabase Privacy Policy → • Stripe — Voluntary donations are processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not store your payment card details on our servers. Stripe handles all payment data in compliance with PCI DSS Level 1 standards. Stripe Privacy Policy → • Apple — If you download and use the Light of Life iOS app, Apple may collect data in accordance with Apple's App Store and platform policies. We do not sell your data to any of these providers for advertising or marketing purposes. Data shared with third-party providers is limited strictly to what is necessary to deliver the requested service feature. 4. Data Retention We retain your personal information for as long as your account remains active. If you request account deletion, your personal data is removed from our active systems within 30 days of processing your request. Certain information may be retained beyond this period where required by law — including financial transaction records, security and fraud prevention data, and data related to active legal matters. Any such retention is strictly limited in scope and duration and is not used for operational or commercial purposes. 5. Data transfers to the United States are conducted under applicable data transfer mechanisms. 6.
